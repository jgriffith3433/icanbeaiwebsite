# Characters: style line and neighbours

## Style line

Stylized 3D cartoon, same family as the Small Weather GLB (Dose Of Destruction art): clean shapes, bold readable
silhouettes, smooth-shaded low-poly. Proportions: about 7 heads tall (1.8 m before `scale`), a slightly large head
(radius 0.18 m, a touch taller than wide), narrow waist, hands and feet simple rounded shapes. Palette: one strong
signature colour per person on the torso, a dark neutral for legs, one accent; skin tones vary. Face style: two dark
oval eyes, a small nose, a short mouth, ears; no outlines. Head and face are never hidden by a hat from the high
camera (brims and helmets sit back and high). Built in code by `js/peoplekit.js`: named nodes `<id>:hipL`, `kneeL`,
`ankleL`, `shoulderR`, `elbowR`, `wristR`, `neck`, `head`.
