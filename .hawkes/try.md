1. Open the beta site, turn Sound on, Take control.
2. Walk (or jump the clock) to the plaza by the fountain and café chairs (east along the avenue, then north into the paving).
3. Hold Wind (V on keyboard, or hold the Wind verb on a phone) until the funnel appears — three grey shells twisting up, dust at the base, chairs and bins spinning with it.
4. Watch them fling out and settle on the paving as the funnel fades; walk back a minute later — the toppled ones are still there.
5. On a phone at low quality: same cast; the funnel uses two shells, fewer flying props, and the frame rate should stay playable.
